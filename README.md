# The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head

Entry for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c): one short line about office life, judged head to head by an AI judge against a published brief.

## The entry

> I accidentally hit reply-all once. HR still introduces me as 'a cautionary tale'.

**81 characters** — within the 1–200 character gate.

Why this joke: the setup is something anyone who has worked in an office recognises instantly — the reply-all accident. The turn is unexpected: instead of the mistake being forgiven or forgotten, HR has turned it into the speaker's permanent introduction. The punchline, "a cautionary tale", lands last, and every word earns its place.

## How it meets the judge's brief

| Criterion | How the entry meets it |
| --- | --- |
| Real one-line joke, setup + turn | Setup ("hit reply-all once") → turn (HR's introduction) |
| Turns on something office workers know | The reply-all accident, a universal office trauma |
| Surprises | The punishment became the speaker's identity |
| Lands in one line, punchline last | 81 characters, punchline is the final phrase |
| Feels new | Original phrasing, not a circulating office joke |
